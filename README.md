# ICICI Bank Probationary Officer (PO) Programme

A quick-reference guide to the ICICI Bank PO Programme — eligibility, selection process, training, salary, and the Relationship Manager role.

> **Status:** Cleared the ICICI Bank PO assessment test ✅

---

## 📌 Overview

The ICICI Bank Probationary Officer Programme is an entry-level hiring track for graduates and postgraduates who want to build a career in banking, sales, and relationship management. Selected candidates go through structured classroom training before being placed as Relationship Managers at ICICI Bank branches across India.

## ✅ Eligibility

| Criteria | Details |
|---|---|
| Qualification | Graduation (any discipline) / B.E. / B.Tech / Post-Graduation |
| Experience | Freshers welcome; candidates with up to 2 years' experience also eligible |
| Mobility | Should be open to relocation and field-based sales/relationship roles |

## 🧭 Selection Process

1. **Online Application** — Apply via the ICICI Bank careers portal.
2. **Online Aptitude Test** — Covers Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General Awareness.
3. **Face-to-Face Interview** — For candidates who clear the aptitude test.
4. **Offer & Onboarding** — Shortlisted candidates are enrolled into the training batch.

## 🎓 Training Programme

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Location | Residential training campus in **Bangalore (Bengaluru)** |
| Duration | 2 months of classroom training |
| Stipend during training | ₹18,000 per month |
| Focus areas | Banking products, sales & relationship management, compliance, branch operations, case studies |

*Note: ICICI also runs a separate, longer-format "ICICI Manipal PO Programme" (in partnership with Manipal Global Education Services) involving a paid diploma course, internships, and a total programme length of several months. This is a distinct, fee-based pathway — different from the direct-hire PO Programme above.*

## 💰 Salary

| Stage | Amount |
|---|---|
| Stipend (during training) | ₹18,000/month |
| Starting CTC (post-training) | ₹4.50 – ₹5.00 LPA (varies by posting location) |

## 🤝 Role After Training: Relationship Manager

On successful completion of training, candidates are placed as **Relationship Managers** at ICICI Bank branches. Core responsibilities include:

- Acquiring new customers and cross-selling banking products
- Managing and growing existing customer relationships
- Field visits for sales and servicing
- Meeting branch-level sales and service targets
- Ensuring compliance with banking norms and processes

## 📄 Disclaimer

Salary, stipend, and programme details are sourced from publicly available ICICI Bank recruitment listings as of 2026 and may change. Please verify current figures on the [official ICICI Bank careers page](https://www.icicibank.com) before making decisions.
